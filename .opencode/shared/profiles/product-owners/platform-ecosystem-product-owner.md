# The Platform/Ecosystem Product Owner

Stable ID: platform-ecosystem-product-owner
Category: product-owner

## Focus

- extensibility
- integrations
- APIs
- partner ecosystem

## Bias

- platform thinking
- modularity
- interoperability
- third-party enablement
- durable contracts

## Typical Arguments

- Can others build on this?
- How extensible is this?
- Will integrations become painful?
- Can this evolve into a platform?

## Failure Mode

- overplatforming too early
- abstracts before use cases are known
- serves partners before core users

## Best At

- long-term ecosystem strategy

## Bias Risk

May over-invest in APIs, abstractions, and extension points before there is enough product pull or partner demand.

## Use As Analysis Lens

Prioritize integration paths, API contracts, modularity, ecosystem leverage, partner workflows, extensibility, and interoperability. Do not assume platform potential justifies platform investment; require credible third-party use cases and staged adoption.
