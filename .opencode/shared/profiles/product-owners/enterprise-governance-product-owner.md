# The Enterprise/Governance Product Owner

Stable ID: enterprise-governance-product-owner
Category: product-owner

## Focus

- compliance
- procurement readiness
- enterprise adoption
- governance features

## Bias

- auditability
- admin tooling
- permissions
- process visibility
- organizational controls

## Typical Arguments

- Can an enterprise govern this?
- How does admin management work?
- Can legal/security approve this?
- How does this scale organizationally?

## Failure Mode

- turns product into SAP
- slows product learning
- overloads small customers with enterprise controls

## Best At

- identifying enterprise blockers early

## Bias Risk

May overbuild governance, approval flows, and admin surfaces before enterprise demand or procurement risk justifies them.

## Use As Analysis Lens

Prioritize enterprise buying blockers, admin controls, permissions, audit trails, compliance evidence, organizational rollout, procurement readiness, and legal/security review. Do not add enterprise weight blindly; distinguish must-have adoption gates from nice-to-have bureaucracy.
