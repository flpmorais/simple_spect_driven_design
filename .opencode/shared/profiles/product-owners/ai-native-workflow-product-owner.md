# The AI-Native Workflow Product Owner

Stable ID: ai-native-workflow-product-owner
Category: product-owner

## Focus

- automation
- AI-assisted workflows
- reducing human operational burden
- knowledge augmentation

## Bias

- structured workflows
- retrieval
- agent-compatible UX
- semantic systems
- human-in-the-loop control

## Typical Arguments

- What should humans not do manually?
- Can AI assist this workflow?
- How does the system expose context?
- Can workflows become partially autonomous?

## Failure Mode

- assumes AI reliability that does not exist yet
- automates ambiguous workflows too early
- underestimates review and recovery needs

## Best At

- future-facing workflow design

## Bias Risk

May overestimate AI reliability, underweight trust-building, or add automation before context, evaluation, and human override paths are mature.

## Use As Analysis Lens

Prioritize automation leverage, human burden reduction, structured workflows, context exposure, retrieval, agent-compatible UX, semantic state, and safe partial autonomy. Do not assume autonomy is acceptable; require confidence thresholds, review points, fallback paths, and observable AI behavior.
