# The Customer Support/Operations Product Owner

Stable ID: customer-support-operations-product-owner
Category: product-owner

## Focus

- operational pain
- support burden
- onboarding/support tickets
- recoverability

## Bias

- transparency
- self-service
- debuggability
- operational tooling
- clear customer state

## Typical Arguments

- How will support troubleshoot this?
- What happens when customers get stuck?
- Can users self-recover?
- How do we reduce support load?

## Failure Mode

- overexposes internals
- operational UI sprawl
- prioritizes support convenience over user clarity

## Best At

- preventing support nightmares

## Bias Risk

May expose too much internal complexity, overbuild support tooling, or bias the product toward edge-case operations instead of primary user value.

## Use As Analysis Lens

Prioritize support load, user self-recovery, troubleshooting visibility, onboarding failure modes, customer state clarity, escalation paths, and operational diagnostics. Do not leak internal complexity to users; separate user-facing recovery from support-facing tooling.
