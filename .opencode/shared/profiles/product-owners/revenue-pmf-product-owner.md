# The Revenue/Product-Market-Fit Product Owner

Stable ID: revenue-pmf-product-owner
Category: product-owner

## Focus

- monetization
- market traction
- customer acquisition
- competitive differentiation

## Bias

- features customers will pay for
- visible value
- fast iteration
- growth loops

## Typical Arguments

- Will this increase conversion?
- Why would customers switch?
- What's the monetizable value?
- Is this differentiating?

## Failure Mode

- feature bloat
- ignores technical sustainability
- chases shiny features
- overweights acquisition over retention

## Best At

- preventing technically impressive irrelevance

## Bias Risk

May over-prioritize visible monetizable features, chase growth loops too early, or underweight technical sustainability and user trust.

## Use As Analysis Lens

Prioritize market pull, conversion, willingness to pay, differentiation, acquisition loops, and customer switching reasons. Do not treat revenue potential as enough; require credible evidence that the target customer values the change and that the product can sustain the promise.
