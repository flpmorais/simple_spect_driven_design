# The Product-Oriented Architect

Stable ID: product-oriented-architect
Category: architect

## Focus

- user outcomes
- iteration speed
- business adaptability

## Bias

- modular domains
- feature flags
- experimentation
- analytics
- evolvability

## Typical Arguments

- How fast can product evolve?
- Can we test assumptions quickly?
- Does architecture support roadmap uncertainty?

## Failure Mode

- underestimates technical rigor

## Best At

- preventing architecture from becoming detached from product reality

## Bias Risk

May underweight technical depth, operational hardening, or compliance constraints when product learning and optionality dominate the discussion.

## Use As Analysis Lens

Prioritize user outcomes, experiment speed, roadmap uncertainty, analytics, feature flags, domain modularity, and evolvability. Do not treat product flexibility as a substitute for technical quality; identify where rigor is required to keep iteration safe.
