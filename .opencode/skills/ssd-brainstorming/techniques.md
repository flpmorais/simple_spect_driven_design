# SSD Brainstorming Techniques

Category-preserving catalogue derived from the original brainstorming methods. Use stable technique names as IDs. Keep selections small and goal-fit. Prefer caller-provided techniques when supplied; otherwise select from this catalogue.

## collaborative

### Yes And Building
Purpose: build momentum through positive additions where each idea becomes a launch pad for the next.
Covers: collaboration, momentum, additive ideation.
Action: Start from a seed idea; add successive "yes, and" extensions; keep each extension concrete and useful.

### Brain Writing Round Robin
Purpose: generate ideas silently, then pass and build on written concepts.
Covers: collaboration, equal participation, documentation.
Action: Generate independent seed ideas; add one build to each seed; merge the strongest built ideas.

### Random Stimulation
Purpose: use random words or images as creative catalysts for unexpected connections.
Covers: novelty, lateral thinking, stuck points.
Action: Pick unrelated stimuli; force links to the goal; convert useful links into viable ideas.

### Role Playing
Purpose: generate solutions from multiple stakeholder perspectives.
Covers: empathy, stakeholders, comprehensive consideration.
Action: Name relevant roles; generate needs, objections, incentives, and ideas from each role.

### Ideation Relay Race
Purpose: build ideas rapidly under time pressure to prevent overthinking.
Covers: speed, momentum, rapid divergence.
Action: Generate quick ideas in sequence; each idea must build on or sharply differ from the previous one.

## creative

### What If Scenarios
Purpose: explore radical possibilities by questioning constraints and assumptions.
Covers: divergence, possibility space, assumption challenge.
Action: Pose extreme "what if" conditions; generate implications; keep ideas that reveal useful possibilities.

### Analogical Thinking
Purpose: draw parallels to other domains and transfer useful solution patterns.
Covers: analogy, cross-domain transfer, pattern reuse.
Action: Select analogous domains; extract working patterns; adapt those patterns to the goal.

### Reversal Inversion
Purpose: flip problems upside down to reveal hidden assumptions and new angles.
Covers: inversion, assumptions, reframing.
Action: State assumed truths; invert each one; derive practical ideas from productive inversions.

### First Principles Thinking
Purpose: strip away assumptions and rebuild from fundamental truths.
Covers: fundamentals, simplification, innovation.
Action: List known facts; remove inherited assumptions; rebuild solution directions from the facts.

### Forced Relationships
Purpose: connect unrelated concepts to spark new combinations.
Covers: lateral connections, novelty, concept bridging.
Action: Pair the goal with unrelated concepts; identify bridges; turn the strongest bridges into ideas.

### Time Shifting
Purpose: explore solutions across past, present, and future constraints.
Covers: temporal perspective, constraints, future thinking.
Action: Generate ideas for past, near-term, and future contexts; compare what changes across time.

### Metaphor Mapping
Purpose: use extended metaphors to make abstract challenges tangible.
Covers: reframing, narrative, abstraction.
Action: Choose a metaphor; map its parts to the problem; generate ideas from the mapped relationships.

### Cross-Pollination
Purpose: transfer solutions from different industries or domains.
Covers: domain transfer, innovation, adaptation.
Action: Pick distant domains; identify their proven practices; adapt them to the current goal.

### Concept Blending
Purpose: merge concepts to create new categories or hybrid solutions.
Covers: synthesis, category creation, innovation.
Action: Select two or more concepts; combine their essential traits; define the resulting hybrid idea.

### Reverse Brainstorming
Purpose: generate problems or failure paths to reveal solution opportunities.
Covers: risk, inversion, hidden opportunities.
Action: Generate ways to worsen the problem; invert each harmful path into a useful solution idea.

### Sensory Exploration
Purpose: use sensory perspectives to discover non-obvious solution dimensions.
Covers: sensory thinking, experience, multidimensional ideation.
Action: Reframe the goal through sensory experience; generate ideas for how it feels, sounds, looks, or moves.

## deep

### Five Whys
Purpose: drill through causation layers to uncover root causes.
Covers: root cause, problem definition, depth.
Action: Ask why repeatedly for each visible problem; generate ideas that address root causes, not symptoms.

### Morphological Analysis
Purpose: explore parameter combinations across a complex system.
Covers: systems, combinations, solution mapping.
Action: Identify key parameters; list options for each; combine options into distinct solution ideas.

### Provocation Technique
Purpose: use provocative statements to extract useful ideas from absurd starts.
Covers: provocation, breakthrough thinking, reframing.
Action: Create provocative statements; extract useful principles; convert those principles into ideas.

### Assumption Reversal
Purpose: identify and flip core assumptions, then rebuild from new premises.
Covers: assumptions, paradigm shift, critique.
Action: List core assumptions; reverse them; generate ideas that become possible under reversed premises.

### Question Storming
Purpose: generate questions before answers to define the problem space.
Covers: inquiry, problem framing, uncertainty.
Action: Generate important questions only; cluster them; answer the highest-leverage questions with ideas.

### Constraint Mapping
Purpose: identify real and imagined constraints, then find paths around them.
Covers: constraints, feasibility, tradeoffs.
Action: List constraints; mark real versus assumed; generate ideas that remove, route around, or exploit them.

### Failure Analysis
Purpose: study failure modes and past failures to extract useful lessons.
Covers: risk, learning, prevention.
Action: Identify likely failures; infer causes; generate prevention, detection, or recovery ideas.

### Emergent Thinking
Purpose: let patterns and solutions emerge without forcing linear progression.
Covers: complexity, emergence, pattern recognition.
Action: Review existing ideas; identify emerging patterns; generate ideas that amplify promising patterns.

## introspective_delight

### Inner Child Conference
Purpose: channel childhood curiosity and playful questioning.
Covers: curiosity, play, unfiltered questions.
Action: Ask naive and playful questions; remove adult filtering; turn useful questions into ideas.

### Shadow Work Mining
Purpose: explore what is being avoided or resisted to uncover hidden insight.
Covers: resistance, hidden blockers, uncomfortable truths.
Action: Name avoided topics and fears; infer what they reveal; generate ideas that address or use them.

### Values Archaeology
Purpose: excavate underlying values that should guide decisions.
Covers: values, priorities, authenticity.
Action: Identify values behind preferences; rank tensions; generate ideas aligned with the strongest values.

### Future Self Interview
Purpose: seek long-term perspective from an imagined wiser future self.
Covers: long-term thinking, perspective, guidance.
Action: Imagine future hindsight; ask what mattered and what was noise; generate ideas from that advice.

### Body Wisdom Dialogue
Purpose: use physical sensations and gut feelings as ideation signals.
Covers: embodied cognition, intuition, tension.
Action: Identify felt tension or ease; map sensations to concerns; generate ideas that resolve or use them.

### Permission Giving
Purpose: explicitly permit impossible or self-censored ideas.
Covers: creative permission, self-limits, barrier removal.
Action: Name self-imposed limits; suspend them; generate ideas normally blocked by those limits.

## structured

### SCAMPER Method
Purpose: use Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to other uses, Eliminate, and Reverse to improve ideas.
Covers: systematic creativity, product improvement, variation.
Action: Apply each SCAMPER verb to the goal; keep the strongest variations as separate ideas.

### Six Thinking Hats
Purpose: examine a challenge through facts, emotions, benefits, risks, creativity, and process.
Covers: perspectives, decisions, balanced analysis.
Action: Generate observations from each hat; convert useful observations into balanced ideas.

### Mind Mapping
Purpose: branch ideas from a central concept to reveal connections.
Covers: connections, organization, breadth.
Action: Place the goal at center; branch themes and subthemes; turn strong branches into ideas.

### Resource Constraints
Purpose: impose extreme limits to force essential priorities and creative efficiency.
Covers: constraints, prioritization, feasibility.
Action: Apply severe resource limits; identify essentials; generate lean ideas that still work.

### Decision Tree Mapping
Purpose: map decision paths and outcomes to reveal risks and opportunities.
Covers: decisions, consequences, options.
Action: Identify key decisions; map paths and outcomes; generate ideas for high-value branches.

### Solution Matrix
Purpose: grid problem variables against solution approaches to find combinations and gaps.
Covers: structure, combinations, coverage.
Action: Create variable and approach axes; scan intersections; turn promising intersections into ideas.

### Trait Transfer
Purpose: borrow useful traits from successful unrelated solutions.
Covers: adaptation, pattern transfer, improvement.
Action: Select successful references; extract traits; transfer those traits into new ideas for the goal.

## theatrical

### Time Travel Talk Show
Purpose: interview past, present, and future selves for temporal perspective.
Covers: time perspective, identity, hindsight.
Action: Ask past, present, and future perspectives what they notice; generate ideas from their conflicts.

### Alien Anthropologist
Purpose: inspect familiar problems through completely foreign eyes.
Covers: outsider view, assumptions, defamiliarization.
Action: Describe the situation as an outsider; flag strange assumptions; generate ideas from outsider confusion.

### Dream Fusion Laboratory
Purpose: start with impossible fantasy solutions, then reverse-engineer practical steps.
Covers: ambition, backcasting, practicality bridge.
Action: Imagine impossible wins; identify why they appeal; derive practical approximations.

### Emotion Orchestra
Purpose: let different emotions lead separate ideation passes, then harmonize them.
Covers: emotions, perspective, synthesis.
Action: Generate ideas from anger, fear, joy, and hope; synthesize ideas that respect multiple emotions.

### Parallel Universe Cafe
Purpose: explore solutions under alternative reality rules.
Covers: assumptions, alternate systems, creative reframing.
Action: Change a core rule of reality or context; generate ideas that become natural under that rule.

### Persona Journey
Purpose: embody archetypes or personas to access different wisdom.
Covers: personas, role perspective, hidden insight.
Action: Choose relevant personas; generate how each would interpret and solve the problem.

## wild

### Chaos Engineering
Purpose: deliberately break ideas to discover robust solutions.
Covers: stress testing, resilience, failure.
Action: Attack existing ideas with stressors; identify breaking points; generate more resilient variants.

### Guerrilla Gardening Ideas
Purpose: plant unexpected solutions in unlikely places.
Covers: surprise, unconventional deployment, stealth innovation.
Action: Identify unlikely places or channels; plant small ideas there; generate stealthy adoption paths.

### Pirate Code Brainstorm
Purpose: take and remix useful ideas without permission-seeking constraints.
Covers: remixing, rule-bending, opportunism.
Action: Identify useful things to borrow; remix them aggressively; adapt them without over-respecting norms.

### Zombie Apocalypse Planning
Purpose: design for extreme survival scenarios to reveal essentials.
Covers: essentials, resilience, stripped-down value.
Action: Remove normal infrastructure and support; identify essentials; generate survival-grade ideas.

### Drunk History Retelling
Purpose: explain complex ideas with uninhibited simplicity.
Covers: simplification, raw truth, plain language.
Action: Restate the goal in blunt simple language; generate ideas from the simplified truth.

### Anti-Solution
Purpose: make the problem worse to reveal assumptions and solution paths.
Covers: sabotage, inversion, risk.
Action: Design ways to worsen the situation; identify mechanisms; invert them into useful ideas.

### Quantum Superposition
Purpose: hold contradictory solutions simultaneously before choosing.
Covers: contradictions, option tension, decision emergence.
Action: Keep conflicting options alive; identify what each optimizes; generate hybrid or conditional ideas.

### Elemental Forces
Purpose: imagine solutions shaped by earth, fire, water, or air.
Covers: elemental metaphor, primal framing, sensory creativity.
Action: Reframe through elemental qualities; generate ideas that embody stability, transformation, flow, or lightness.

## biomimetic

### Nature's Solutions
Purpose: study how nature solves analogous problems.
Covers: biomimicry, adaptation, resilient patterns.
Action: Find natural analogues; extract biological strategies; adapt them into ideas.

### Ecosystem Thinking
Purpose: treat the problem as an ecosystem of relationships and dependencies.
Covers: systems, symbiosis, ecology.
Action: Map actors and exchanges; identify symbiosis or imbalance; generate ecosystem-level ideas.

### Evolutionary Pressure
Purpose: apply selection and adaptation pressure to improve solutions over time.
Covers: iteration, adaptation, optimization.
Action: Define selection pressures; mutate ideas; keep variants that best adapt to pressure.

## quantum

### Observer Effect
Purpose: explore how observing or measuring a solution changes behavior.
Covers: measurement effects, behavior change, feedback.
Action: Identify what gets observed; predict behavior changes; generate ideas that use measurement effects.

### Entanglement Thinking
Purpose: identify hidden connections between distant solution elements.
Covers: dependencies, hidden relationships, systems.
Action: List distant elements; infer hidden dependencies; generate ideas from those connections.

### Superposition Collapse
Purpose: hold multiple potential solutions until constraints force selection.
Covers: options, constraints, convergence.
Action: Keep several options open; apply constraints one by one; generate the solution that survives.

## cultural

### Indigenous Wisdom
Purpose: consider traditional knowledge systems and overlooked cultural approaches.
Covers: traditional knowledge, cultural perspective, stewardship.
Action: Identify relevant traditional principles; translate respectfully into design constraints or ideas.

### Fusion Cuisine
Purpose: mix cultural approaches to create hybrid solutions.
Covers: cultural blending, hybridity, cross-pollination.
Action: Select two cultural approaches; combine their practices or values; generate hybrid ideas.

### Ritual Innovation
Purpose: apply ritual design principles to create transformative experiences.
Covers: ceremony, transformation, experience design.
Action: Identify desired transformation; design symbolic steps; generate ideas that make the change felt.

### Mythic Frameworks
Purpose: use myths and archetypal stories as problem-solving frames.
Covers: archetypes, narrative, collective meaning.
Action: Choose a relevant mythic pattern; map roles and conflict; generate ideas from the story structure.
